Little tips that make a big difference

  • Multiplication means equal groups. For example, 3 × 4 means 3 groups of 4.
  • Division means sharing equally. For example, 12 ÷ 3 means sharing 12 into 3 equal groups.
  • Use the reverse fact. If 6 × 4 = 24, then 24 ÷ 6 = 4 and 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
  • Practise a little every day. Say the question, think of the answer, then check it.
  • Remember zero. Any number multiplied by 0 equals 0. Division by 0 is not defined.
Learning tip: Start with a small range, get confident, and then challenge yourself with bigger numbers.
